## 1. Purpose

This Agreement authorizes contributions from named Company personnel and grants
rights needed to distribute those contributions under open-source and commercial

commit. The parties must decide with counsel whether individual CLAs are also

Company will maintain Schedule A listing employees and other personnel it is

authority.

## 3. Contribution

**Contribution** means any original work of authorship, modification, code,
documentation, design, test, or other material intentionally submitted to a
project-controlled channel by an Authorized Contributor for inclusion in
VFM-engine. Material clearly designated in writing as “Not a Contribution” is
excluded.

applicable law and their relationship.

For every Contribution in which Company owns or controls rights, Company grants
the Project Steward and its successors and assigns a perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free, fully paid-up, transferable, and
sublicensable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of,

license, relicense, and otherwise exploit the Contribution, alone or combined
with other material, under any open-source, source-available, or commercial
license terms, including terms different from those applying at submission.

This grant supports commercial operators and downstream licensees but does not
transfer Company's copyright ownership.

## 6. Company representations and obligations

Company represents and agrees that:

- it has authority to enter the Agreement and grant the stated rights;
- it owns or controls the necessary rights in each covered Contribution;
- Authorized Contributors have signed enforceable invention, copyright, and
  confidentiality terms sufficient for the grants;

joint-venture personnel unless Company can legally bind the relevant rights.

## 7. No implied rights or reciprocal commercial license

This Agreement grants rights in Contributions to the Project Steward. It does
not grant Company a commercial license, source-access right, exclusivity,
governance right, ownership of VFM-engine, or ownership of other contributors'
work. Any commercial license or services relationship requires a separate
agreement.

Project Steward to exercise the granted rights. Non-waivable rights remain
subject to applicable law.

The Project Steward need not accept, use, or distribute any Contribution and may
modify or remove it. Except for express representations, Contributions are
provided on an “as is” basis without support obligations to the extent permitted
by law.
