# Governance

## Stewardship

Qi Li is the founder and current project steward of VFM-engine. The steward is
responsible for the canonical repository, release decisions, roadmap, project
name and branding, contribution acceptance, security response, and licensing
decisions for material the steward is legally entitled to license.

Project stewardship does not erase the copyright of outside contributors.
Accepted contributions remain subject to their recorded copyright and license
grants.

## Decision process

Technical decisions should be made transparently through issues, pull requests,
design notes, or recorded maintainer decisions. The steward may appoint
maintainers and delegate review or release work, but retains final responsibility
unless governance is amended in writing.

Acceptance of a contribution depends on technical merit, project fit, security,
provenance, licensing, and maintainability. There is no entitlement to merge.

## Project assets that are not transferred by participation

Submitting code, purchasing services, receiving a commercial license, or
sponsoring development does not by itself transfer or create joint ownership of:

- the VFM-engine project or canonical repository;
- the project roadmap, release authority, or governance;
- the VFM-engine name, marks, domains, or community accounts;
- Qi Li's pre-existing software, know-how, or copyright; or
- generic core improvements, except where a signed written agreement expressly
  provides otherwise.

## Commercial operator

The recommended present structure is that Qi Li retains the core VFM-engine
copyright. A future company may operate sales, support, services, and commercial
licensing under a written authorization from Qi Li. Formation, financing, or a
change of control of that company should not automatically transfer all present
or future VFM-engine copyright.

Any master commercialization license to such a company should define scope,
sublicensing authority, quality control, termination, and change-of-control
consequences. It should be reviewed independently before signature.

## Amendments

Governance changes apply prospectively. They cannot revoke previously granted
MIT rights or rewrite the ownership of accepted contributions. Material changes
should be documented in the Git history and release notes.
